Privacy Policy for Habit Reveal

Last updated: August 17, 2026
Summary: Habit Reveal is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data. All habit tracking data is stored locally on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Habit Reveal operates as an offline-first application. We do not collect or transmit:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, address).
  • Habit names, completion timestamps, moods, notes, or photos.
  • Location data or biometric data.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All data you create in Habit Reveal (habits, journals, progress, and settings) is stored exclusively on your device's local storage (SharedPreferences / LocalStorage). If you uninstall the app or clear app data, this information will be permanently deleted unless you choose to back it up.

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

If you purchase the Pro version (a one-time purchase), transactions are securely processed directly by the Google Play Store. We do not collect or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.

4. Notifications

Habit Reveal uses local system notifications to remind you of your daily habits at your scheduled time. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not connect to external servers.

5. Third-Party Services

Habit Reveal does not use third-party analytics or advertising networks. The only third-party service used is Google Play Billing API for handling optional Pro upgrades.

6. Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect personal information from children or any other users. It is safe for users of all ages.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
