Privacy Policy for Habit Reveal

Last updated: October 12, 2026
Summary: Habit Reveal works fully offline without an account. Your habits stay on your device unless you choose to sign in (for backup / sync) or use the Couple, Group or Chat features. We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used (for example, whether new users finish the welcome guide). We do not show ads, do not use the Advertising ID, and do not sell your data.

1. Using the App Without an Account

Without signing in, all data you create — habits, progress, moods, notes, journal entries, photos and settings — is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes this information permanently, unless you have made a backup.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, biometric data, or payment card details.

3. Advertising

Habit Reveal contains no ads. The app does not access the Android Advertising ID, and ad personalization and ad-related data collection are disabled in Firebase Analytics.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Pro purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive only the purchase status needed to unlock Pro features; we never see or store your payment details.

5. Notifications

Daily habit reminders are scheduled locally on your device. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in Android settings.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following Google services to operate the app. Their handling of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security:

  • Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication — account sign-in
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage — backup, sync, Couple / Group / Chat features
  • Google Drive API (app data folder only) — optional image backup in your own Drive
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage analytics
  • Google Play Billing — in-app purchases

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone for their own marketing purposes.

7. Data Security

All data sent between the app and Google/Firebase services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Access to cloud data is restricted to your own account and, for shared features, to the members you invited.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Local data: deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Drive images: you can remove them at any time from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Habit Reveal → Delete hidden app data.
  • Account and cloud data: kept while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time inside the app: Settings → Account & Cloud backup → Delete account & cloud data. This permanently removes your cloud backup, Drive photo backup, profile, invite codes, chat messages and your check-ins in couples/groups, removes you from those couples/groups, and deletes your sign-in account. Habits stored only on your phone are kept. If you no longer have the app installed, email us at kiennt.lsn@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account” from the Google account you signed in with; we will complete the deletion within 30 days.
  • Analytics data: automatically deleted by Firebase after 14 months.

9. Your Choices

  • Use the app without signing in — no account or habit data leaves your device (only anonymous analytics and the push token).
  • Sign out at any time to stop syncing.
  • Leave a couple or group at any time.
  • Request a copy or deletion of your data by contacting us (see section 11).

10. Children's Privacy

Habit Reveal is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes and Contact

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time; changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
